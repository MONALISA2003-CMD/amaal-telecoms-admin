## Design decisions derived from research
- Keep cart and checkout concise and transparent.
- Keep product information close to the decision point and avoid invented specifications.
- Make mobile the primary interaction target.
- Keep totals, availability and next actions obvious.
- Use server authority for commercial state while keeping the interface fast.
- Treat product imagery, variation selection, specification clarity and cross-navigation as first-class product-page concerns.
- Do not build payment rails before provider contracts, credentials, webhook verification and reconciliation requirements are ready.
