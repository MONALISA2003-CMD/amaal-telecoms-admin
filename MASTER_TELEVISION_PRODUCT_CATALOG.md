# SOURCE OF TRUTH

Version: 1.0
Last Verified: 2026-08-28

## PURPOSE

Canonical catalogue structure for television products used by Amaal Telecoms. This is the catalogue reference, not physical inventory or pricing.

## SOURCE-OF-TRUTH RULES

- Exact manufacturer model number is authoritative when supplied.
- Do not invent manufacturer SKUs from retailer names.
- Where the supplied source gives a family/size without an exact regional SKU, keep it PARTIALLY_VERIFIED rather than presenting it as an exact manufacturer SKU.
- Market, generation, verification and inventory/catalog status are separate concepts.
- Prices and physical stock do not belong to master product identity.
- One canonical product record per manufacturer model/family; suppliers do not create duplicates.

## PRODUCT DATA MODEL

PostgreSQL stores canonical product identity in `products`, commercial variants in `product_variants`, and the richer TV source-of-truth metadata in the existing `products.specifications` JSON field. Prices and inventory remain separate concerns.

## CRITICAL SEPARATION

CATALOG → what products Amaal recognizes.

INVENTORY → what Amaal currently owns.

SUPPLIER → who can supply the product.

PRICING → what Amaal paid/sells it for.

PUBLIC WEBSITE → approved customer-visible catalogue information.

ADMIN CONSOLE → authorized management surface.
