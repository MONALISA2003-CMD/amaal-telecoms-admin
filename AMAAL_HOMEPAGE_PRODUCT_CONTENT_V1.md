## Research notes

- Apple official specifications confirm iPhone 16 Pro Max has a 256GB capacity option and was introduced in 2024.
- Google official specifications confirm Pixel 9 has a 256GB / 12GB configuration and Tensor G4.
- Samsung official specifications confirm Galaxy A17 4GB / 128GB and a 6.7-inch Super AMOLED display; Samsung also lists a 50MP rear camera with OIS.
- Samsung official specifications confirm Galaxy A07 4GB / 64GB and microSD expansion.
- Samsung official specifications confirm HW-B400F is a 2025 2.0-channel soundbar with built-in woofer, Bluetooth, HDMI ARC, Surround Sound Expansion and Voice Enhance Mode.
- Google official specifications confirm Pixel 11 Pro XL has a 6.8-inch LTPO OLED Super Actua display, Tensor G6, 30+ hour battery life and a 256GB option.
- Samsung official 2026 materials confirm Galaxy Z Fold8 has 256GB, 512GB and 1TB options, with 256GB and 512GB variants using 12GB memory.
- Samsung official materials identify the 512GB special-edition foldable as Galaxy Z Fold Special Edition. The supplied phrase “Galaxy Z fold passport” has therefore been normalised to the official product name rather than inventing a model called “Passport”.
- TECNO official specifications confirm CAMON 50 Pro 5G with Dimensity 7400 Ultimate, 6.78-inch 144Hz 1.5K AMOLED, 256GB options, 50MP OIS camera and 6500mAh battery.
- Infinix official product information confirms Smart 20 configurations including 64GB + 4GB RAM, Helio G81 Ultimate and expandable storage.
- TCL official specifications confirm the C655 family as QLED 4K Google TV with QLED Pro, AiPQ Pro, Dolby Vision/Atmos and gaming features; 75C655 is the 75-inch model.
- Hisense official product information confirms WFQP8014EVMT as an 8kg front loader with inverter technology, steam care and 15-minute quick wash. Regional listings differ on spin speed, so Amaal's supplied 1200 RPM value is retained for the homepage.
- The TCL V635 wording supplied by Amaal is retained as a merchandising name; exact regional V635 specification should be confirmed against the physical Amaal unit before publishing the final detailed specification sheet.
