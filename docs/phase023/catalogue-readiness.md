# Amaal catalogue readiness

The public catalogue is database authoritative. A product is customer publishable only when it has an Active status, an assigned brand and category, a non empty description, an Active variant with a positive selling price, Active Public managed product media, and complete SEO title and description.

Existing records are not automatically published. This prevents unverified prices, missing product photography, and incomplete merchandising from appearing on the customer website.

Business Console now provides an enrichment workflow for existing records. Enrichment accepts product id, slug, or SKU and can update authoritative selling price, SEO fields, description, brand, category, status, and website visibility. Validation can be run before saving.

No price, image, specification, promotion, stock quantity, or other commercial fact is generated by the system.

Payment remains deferred and untouched.
